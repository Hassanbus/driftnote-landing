Privacy

Driftnote is a fictional product and this website is a demo project. The short version: this site does not collect, send or store your personal data.

What this site does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, ads or trackers.
  • No fonts, images or scripts from other websites. Every file comes from this site.

The newsletter form

The form on the home page is a demo. Your email address is checked inside your browser and then thrown away. It is not sent to a server and it is not saved on your device.

What is saved in your browser

One small thing: your choice of light or dark mode. It is saved in your browser's localStorage under the name driftnote-theme, with the value light or dark. It never leaves your device. You can delete it by clearing the site data in your browser settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. To deliver a page, any web host has to process technical data such as your IP address. This project adds nothing on top of that. For details, read the Vercel privacy notice.

Questions

There is no company behind Driftnote. The source code of this site is public, so you can check all of this yourself.
