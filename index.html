Features

Made for writing, not for filing

Six simple ideas that keep writing fast and finding easy.

  • Fast capture

    Open Driftnote and start typing. There is no folder to pick first.

  • Plain-text Markdown

    Notes are plain text with simple Markdown, so they stay easy to read anywhere.

  • Tags and notebooks

    Group notes with tags and notebooks instead of deep folder trees.

  • Search as you type

    Results show up while you type, with the matching words highlighted.

  • Works offline

    Keep writing without a connection. Your notes sync when you are back online.

  • Keyboard first

    Every action has a shortcut, so your hands can stay on the keys.
